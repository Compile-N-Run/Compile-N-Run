# Attention

Attention is the operation that lets every token look at every other token, and it is the heart of every modern LLM. This section derives it from first principles, implements it in PyTorch, and follows it through multi-head, grouped-query and latent variants to its quadratic cost, for anyone who wants to read a model's config.json and know exactly what each attention field means.
