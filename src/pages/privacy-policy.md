# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 3, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Compile N Run** ("we", "us", or "our"), operator of [https://www.compilenrun.com](https://www.compilenrun.com) (the "Site"), collects, uses, and shares information when you visit the Site. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## Information We Collect

Compile N Run is primarily a free educational content website. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for personal information such as your name or address to access our tutorials.

We may collect the following types of information automatically:

- **Usage and device data** — such as your IP address (often anonymized or truncated by our providers), browser type, device type, operating system, referring pages, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit.
- **Cookies and similar technologies** — small data files stored on your device that help us understand site usage and that enable advertising. See the [Cookies](#cookies) section below.

## How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Operate, maintain, and improve the Site and its content.
- Understand how visitors use the Site so we can prioritize and improve our tutorials.
- Display advertising that helps keep the Site free.
- Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse.

## Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. We and our third-party partners use cookies to analyze traffic and to serve advertising. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the Site may not function properly without them.

## Advertising and Google AdSense

We use **Google AdSense**, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements on the Site.

- Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on the Site.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- Third-party vendors and ad networks may also use cookies to serve ads.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting **Google Ads Settings** at [https://adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting [https://www.aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices).

For more information about how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps, please see [https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Analytics

To understand how our content is used and to improve it, we use privacy-conscious analytics services, which may include:

- **Umami Analytics** — a privacy-focused analytics tool that does not use cookies to track individuals across sites.
- **Microsoft Clarity** — which helps us understand how visitors interact with the Site through usage metrics and aggregated session data.
- **Google services** — which may collect usage statistics as described above.

These services collect aggregated, non-identifying usage information that helps us improve the Site.

## Third-Party Links

Our Site contains links to external websites and resources that we do not operate or control. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third-party sites, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any site you visit.

## Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

## Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it, and the right to opt out of personalized advertising. To exercise any of these rights, or to ask a question about your data, please contact us using the details below.

## Data Retention and Security

We retain automatically collected data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. While no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, we take reasonable measures to protect the information processed through the Site.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we handle information.
